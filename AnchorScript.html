Wedding MC Script

Welcome, ceremony flow and family introductions

Part 1: Welcome & Ceremony Flow

Welcome

“A very good [afternoon/evening] to all of you! It’s wonderful to see so many smiling faces here. Together with the families of [Couple’s Names], I’d like to thank you for coming to share in their happiness today.”

Introduction

“For those who don’t know me, I’m [MC’s Name], and I’ll be guiding you through the celebration this [afternoon/evening]. I’m truly happy to be here as [Couple’s Names] begin this new chapter.”

Acknowledgment

“A special word of thanks to the elders, relatives, and friends who’ve travelled from near and far. Having all of you here is the greatest gift [Couple’s Names] could ask for, and they carry your blessings with them today.”

Couple’s Grand Entry

“Friends, the time has come. Would you all please stand and welcome, with your warmest applause, Mr. and Mrs. [NAME]!”

[Let the applause build]

“Here they come, ready to begin their journey together.”

Couple and Family on Stage

“I now invite [Bride’s Name] and [Groom’s Name] to step onto the stage.”

[Wait for the couple to take their place]

“And may I call the bride’s and groom’s parents and siblings to join them, to stand beside the couple at this special moment.”

Prayer and Blessing

“Every good beginning rests on God’s grace, and every marriage is held together by His blessing.”

“I request Achen [NAME OF PRIEST] to please come up to the stage and lead us in prayer.”

[Wait for Achen to reach the stage]

“Let us all bow our heads, asking God’s blessings on this marriage and on the meal we are about to enjoy together.”

Welcome Drink

“May I ask [Groom’s Mother’s Name] and [Bride’s Mother’s Name] to step forward. Each mother will now offer the welcome drink to her new son or daughter-in-law, a warm gesture that brings the two families closer. A round of applause, please!”

Lighting of the Unity Candle

“I invite the parents of both families to light the two candles on either side. After that, [Bride’s Name] and [Groom’s Name] will come together to light the centre candle.”

Optional closing line:

“May this flame bring them comfort on difficult days, courage when they need it, and happiness through every season of life.”

Cake Cutting

“Now for the part everyone loves. Let’s invite the couple to the cake table.”

[As they get ready]

“Cameras ready, everyone! As they cut the first slice together, may their life be as sweet as the cake, and full of love and togetherness.”

Part 2: Family Introductions & Transition

Introducing the Groom

“Allow me to tell you a little about the groom. [Groom’s Name] is from the [Family Name] family, the much-loved son of Mr. and Mrs. [Parents’ Names]. After completing [Education Details] at [Institution], he now works as a [Designation] with [Company Name] in [Location].”

“He has a [brother/sister], [Sibling’s Name], who is pursuing [Education Details] at [Institution], [Location]. His father, Mr. [Groom’s Father’s Name], is a [Designation] at [Company Name], and his mother, Mrs. [Groom’s Mother’s Name], is a [Designation] at [Company Name], both in [Location].”

Introducing the Bride

“And now a few words about the bride. [Bride’s Name] belongs to the [Family Name] family, the cherished daughter of Mr. and Mrs. [Parents’ Names]. She earned her [Education Details] from [Institution] and works as a [Designation] at [Company Name] in [Location].”

“Her father, Mr. [Bride’s Father’s Name], is a [Designation] at [Company Name], and her mother, Mrs. [Bride’s Mother’s Name], is a [Designation] at [Company Name], both in [Location].”

Outfit Change Announcement

“The couple will take a short break now to change into their next outfits. Please do enjoy your meal and stay seated comfortably. They’ll be back with us very soon, and we have more lovely moments ahead!”
